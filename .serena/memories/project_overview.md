# CurHub - Curriculum Management System

## Project Purpose
CurHub is a Django-based web application for managing university curriculum and educational programs. The system is designed to handle:

- **Chương trình đào tạo (CTDT)** - Training/Educational Programs
- **Học phần** - Course/Subject Management
- **Đề cương học phần** - Course Syllabi
- **Chuẩn đầu ra** - Learning Outcomes/Standards
- **Đơn vị đào tao** - Training Units/Departments
- **Mục tiêu đào tạo** - Training Objectives

## Key Features
- Comprehensive curriculum management
- Course and subject tracking
- Learning outcome mapping
- Department and unit management
- Syllabus creation and management
- Vietnamese language support with UTF-8 encoding
- User authentication and authorization
- Data import/export capabilities

## Target Users
University administrators, academic staff, and curriculum managers who need to manage educational programs and course structures.
